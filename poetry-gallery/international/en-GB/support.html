Chinese Poetry Gallery · Support

Version 1.2.0 includes 365 classical Chinese poems. Read the poems, view their illustrations and listen to prerecorded AI narration in Mandarin offline. No sign-in is required.

Change language and reading mode in Settings. Browse the Library by theme, poet or poetic form.

If audio is silent, check media volume and the output device. Resume reading from the Library.

For help, include the app version, iOS version and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

When ads appear

The Library list may show cards labelled as ads. Once you have finished at least five different poems and spent a total of three minutes actively reading, a full-screen ad may appear when you choose the next poem or return to the Library. Full-screen ads are at least 60 seconds apart. Reading a poem, listening to narration and earning a daily stamp do not trigger an ad.
