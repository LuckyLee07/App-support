Park & Blast privacy policy

Updated November 12, 2026 · Published by Lee

Game progress

Park & Blast saves progress and settings on your device. The game does not require an account or send your level history to a developer-operated server. Removing the app can erase its local data.

Advertising on iOS

The game uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between completed levels. Google's User Messaging Platform manages applicable consent notices and choices. Where available, open Privacy choices on the home screen to review your selection.

Google's services may process your IP address to estimate approximate location, device identifiers, interactions with the app and ads, crash logs, performance and other diagnostics. These data support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, analytics and operation of the service. The game requests non-personalized ads, disables the publisher first-party ID, and does not request permission to track across apps. Google describes its data use and retention in its privacy policy.

Support messages

If you email us, Lee receives your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to reply and investigate the issue and retain them for as long as needed to handle the request. You can use the same address to ask about or request deletion of a support message.

Visiting this website

GitHub Pages hosts these pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and request logs to serve and secure the website. See the GitHub privacy statement. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts.
