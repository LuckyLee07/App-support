Park & Blast support

How do I clear a level?

Tap a tank when the route in front of it is clear. It moves into an open firing bay and shoots enemies of the same color. Check the incoming enemies before you fill your last bay. Complete a level to unlock the next one.

Where is my progress?

Your unlocked levels and settings are saved on this device. You can play without an account. Deleting the app can remove your progress.

How do ad rewards work?

When offered, you can choose to watch a video to open an extra bay for the level, use a tool, or revive. The reward is granted when the ad network confirms you earned it. If a video is unavailable, check your connection and try again later. A full-screen ad may also appear when you choose NEXT LEVEL after a win.
